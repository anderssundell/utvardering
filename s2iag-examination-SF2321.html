Grading form, Method thesis 13 1 Overview This course provides students with a comprehensive understanding of the design and application of methods for causal inference using the most common regression techniques featured in social science research and commercial analyses and reports. The course also teaches students how to conduct statistical analysis using Stata, and how to interpret the empirical results from statistical analyses. We further emphasize students’ learning by letting students independently choose research questions, data sets and variables for several of the assignments. The course is divided into three main parts. The first focuses on theory and research design for causal inference (assignment 1) but also offers recap lectures and an introduction to the statistical software Stata. The aim with the lecture and the subsequent Stata tutorial is to provide an opportunity to revise basic statistical analysis, to start get to know Stata and to get everyone up to speed. The second, and largest part of the course, is devoted to lectures, teacher-led tutorials, and assignments on linear regression and the empirical application of statistical research methods for causal inference (assignment 2-6). The third part of the course is focused on an independent research paper, the Method thesis (assignment 7), in which learned skills are put into practice and the design and statistical methods for causal inference are applied on a self-chosen research problem and data material. Learning outcomes After completing the course, the students shall reach the following learning outcomes: Knowledge and understanding • Display knowledge and understanding of how to design social science research projects in order to make causal inferences. • Display knowledge and understanding of the empirical application of statistical research methods for causal inference aimed to answer various research questions in the social sciences. • Display comprehensive knowledge about the usage of different statistical techniques, primarily multivariate regression techniques. Competence and skills • Be able to independently use methods of data management and to apply various statistical techniques on different data sources using a statistical computer package. • Be able to independently design and statistically test theoretically deduced causal models. • Demonstrate good ability to correctly present, visualize, interpret and analyze empirical results obtained through different statistical methods. • Independently plan and carry out a smaller study to solve a causal inference problem using appropriate statistical methods in a research paper. 2 • Independently produce text in accordance with good academic practice, including proper citation technique and use of references • Communicate clearly and proficiently in English both orally and in writing Judgement and approach • Independently and critically assess research designs, methodology, empirical analyses and conclusions from social science research based on statistical analysis. • Independently and critically assess and evaluate principles of operationalization, validity and reliability. • Identify, evaluate and judge problems of research ethics in Social Sciences. Examination and course requirements The course is examined by completing one (1) oral group assignment (1 hec), five (5) written group assignments (1 to 3 hec each, in total 8 hec), and by independently writing and orally presenting a Method thesis (6 hec). To be awarded Pass, assignments 1-6 must be awarded Pass and assignment 7 (the Method thesis) must be awarded Pass. To be awarded Pass with Distinction, assignments 1-6 must be awarded Pass and assignment 7 (the Method thesis) must be awarded Pass with Distinction. To reach Pass, the assignments must be correctly carried out, you must understand the application of the statistical method(s) in question, you must present your work according to research standards and you must draw correct conclusions. To reach Pass with Distinction, apart from the requirements for Pass, you also need to adopt an independent approach in the Method thesis including a well-motivated theoretical model, critically assess the chosen course of action by actively discussing strengths and weaknesses regarding the causal inference design, operationalization, analytic techniques, conclusions and alternative procedures. We grade and provide written feedback on all written assignments through Canvas. For assignment 2-6, the grading teacher could comment directly in your submitted assignment on Canvas, and/or summarize the feedback in a comment to your assignment. Assignment 7, The Method Thesis The last three weeks of the course work are devoted to independent work. The Method thesis involves planning the carrying out of a smaller study to solve a causal inference problem using appropriate statistical methods in a research paper. The method thesis resembles a master’s thesis although much shorter and with empirical and methodological emphasis. To support 4 your work with the Method thesis, one of the instructor will be assigned as your advisor and you will be able to receive feedback on your initial idea (the abstract) and on your draft. We also offer a short Stata help desk session during the work with the method thesis. The method thesis will be presented by each student at the final seminar. Read more about the method thesis in the Assignment 7, method thesis instructions, uploaded on Canvas.
