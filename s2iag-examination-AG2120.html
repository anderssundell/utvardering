Grading
grading of the assignment will be affected negatively. Did not submit a seminar assignment on time: If you submit your assignments after the deadline, this will affect the grading negatively. You will need to submit the assignment in a “late/revised” submission box by 21 February 2026. Did not participate in a seminar: Contact the instructor of the seminar you missed. You will need to complete a compensation oral assignment. Did not make a seminar presentation: You will need to do a 5-6 minute makeup presentation of your brief/paper using PowerPoint or similar visual aid. Contact the instructor of the seminar you missed by latest 21 February 2026 for instructions. Final paper and student conference absences/missing assignments: Did not submit final paper abstract on time: If you do not submit your abstract by 5pm, 12 December, you will not be allowed to participate in the student conference on 18 December 2025. We want to ensure all students are well-equipped to participate in the group work. Did not participate in student conference: If you are not able to participate in the student conference on 18 December 2025, you will need to do a make-up presentation of your country brief (5-8 minutes). You will also need to do an oral assignment. Contact the instructor responsible for your assigned country group by latest 21 February 2026. Did not submit final research paper on time: You will need to submit your paper by 21 February 2026, in the late/revised box. In addition, you will need to present your paper (5-8 minutes) and do an oral assignment. Contact the instructor responsible for your assigned country group by latest 21 February 2026.
